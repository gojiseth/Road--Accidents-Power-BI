## Insights & Recommendations - 7 Charts Analysis

### 1. KPIs - Overall Casualty Performance
**Insight:** 121.3K total casualties with 0.8K fatalities = 0.66% fatal rate. Serious + Fatal = 15K high-impact cases = 12.4% of all casualties. Fatalities dropped 44% YoY but serious cases up 4%.  
**Recommendation:** Apply "Vision Zero" approach. Prioritize 80% safety budget on reducing 15K serious/fatal cases. Target: <0.7K fatalities next year.

### 2. Casualties by Vehicles
**Insight:** Cars dominate with 95,720 casualties = ~79% of total. Motorcycles: 10,104. Pedestrians: 10,104. Cyclists: 4,548. HGVs: 4,548. Volume ≠ risk - motorcycles/pedestrians likely highest rate per mile.  
**Recommendation:** Segment safety campaigns by road user. Motorcycles: advanced rider training + airbag vests. Pedestrians/Cyclists: 20mph zones + protected lanes. Cars: fatigue + distraction awareness.

### 3. Monthly Trend CY vs PY Casualties
**Insight:** Line chart shows CY 2022 consistently above PY 2021 all year. Peak Aug-Oct at 11K-12K casualties. Low Jan-Mar at 7K-9K. Example: Feb 2022 9,142 vs 2021 8,681. Dec 2022 7,877 vs 2021 11,028.  
**Recommendation:** Seasonal strategy: Deploy extra traffic police Jun-Oct before peak. Winter Dec-Feb: focus on weather driving + reduced daylight safety. Target: bring CY trend below PY by Q4.

### 4. Casualties by Road Type
**Insight:** Bar chart shows Single carriageway >> Dual carriageway > Roundabout > One-way street > Slip road. Single lanes cause majority of casualties due to no central barrier + higher rural speeds.  
**Recommendation:** Safety audit all high-casualty single carriageways. Add center line rumble strips + overtaking lanes. Reduce rural single lane limits 60mph→50mph. Convert busiest singles to dual carriageway.

### 5. Casualties by Urban/Rural
**Insight:** Rural 61.23% vs Urban 38.77%. Rural roads cause 3 out of 5 casualties despite lower traffic density. Higher speeds + longer emergency response times increase severity in rural areas.  
**Recommendation:** Rural Road Safety Fund: average speed cameras, verge cutting for sightlines, barrier upgrades on bends. Urban: 20mph zones + pedestrian crossings. Launch "Arrive Alive Rural" campaign.

### 6. Casualties by Light Conditions
**Insight:** Day 55.65% vs Darkness 44.35%. Darkness = 44% of casualties but only ~25-30% of driving hours. Night risk per hour = 1.7x higher than daytime due to reduced visibility.  
**Recommendation:** Night safety upgrades: reflective road markings, cat's eyes, street lighting on A-roads. Public campaign "Be Visible" for pedestrians/cyclists. Mandatory vehicle light checks Oct-Mar.

### 7. Casualties and Accidents by Location - Local Authority Hotspots
**Insight:** UK map shows massive geographic inequality. Top hotspots from map tooltips: York 677 casualties, South Tyneside 671, Knowsley 637, Sheffield 397, Fenland 355. Lowest: Sleaford 10, Oadby 14. Top LA has 67x more casualties than bottom. Clustering in North England + Midlands.  
**Recommendation:** "Top 10 Hotspot Initiative": Dedicated funding + road engineers for York, South Tyneside, Knowsley. Interventions: junction redesigns, speed camera networks, school street closures. Benchmark Sleaford/Oadby practices and replicate nationally.

## Conclusion & H2 Action Plan

**CY 2022 Summary:** 121.3K casualties, 0.8K fatal. Fatalities down 44% YoY but serious casualties up 4%. 

**4 Critical Risks Identified:**
1. **Rural Risk:** 61% casualties on rural roads with higher severity outcomes
2. **Night Risk:** 44% casualties in darkness at 1.7x hourly risk rate
3. **Infrastructure Risk:** Single carriageways = deadliest road type
4. **Geographic Risk:** Top 3 LAs = 1,985 casualties vs bottom 3 = 33 casualties

**H2 Target:** Reduce serious+fatal casualties by 15% YoY, fatalities <0.7K

**4-Pillar Action Plan:**
1. **Rural Focus:** Safety upgrades + speed management on A/B roads
2. **Night Focus:** Lighting + visibility tech + "Be Seen" campaign
3. **Road Type Focus:** Fix single carriageway hotspots with barriers + speed limits  
4. **Geographic Focus:** Deploy resources to Top 10 Local Authorities first
